# Applications

Website: lead with category and Abu Dhabi origin. Product onboarding: ask what matters before showing results. City reports: use comparable criteria and experience-economy language. Sales decks: demonstrate improvement and feedback ROI with evidence. Social: educate on criteria, fit and trends. Events: speak to experience economy leaders.

Reports use city snapshot, sector performance, horizontal benchmark, strengths and gaps, opportunities, and action roadmap.
