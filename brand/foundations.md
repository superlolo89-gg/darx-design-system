# Strategic foundations

Beyond ratings. Find what fits.

## Purpose

To make complex experience information meaningful, comparable and useful for every person, business and city.

## Mission

To transform fragmented ratings, reviews, data and feedback into impartial, explainable intelligence that helps people choose better and organizations improve faster.

## Vision

One universal standard for how experience quality is measured. Universal in method. Local in judgement.

## Promise

We turn many signals into the right fit, the right action and the right benchmark, powered by the principles of Emotional Infrastructure.
