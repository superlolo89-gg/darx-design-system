# Language

Use concise, clear, warm and evidence-led language. Explain relevant criteria, reasons, uncertainty and trade-offs.

Prefer right fit, relevant criteria, impartial evidence, explainable recommendation, city benchmark and experience improvement. Avoid fully objective truth, perfect experience, revolutionary algorithm, black-box AI and universal best claims.

No paid relationship may influence evaluation without prominent disclosure and methodological separation. Never imply access to every source or replacement of existing guides.

Primary recommended tagline: Beyond ratings. Find what fits.

Source: Brand Book pp. 6, 22–23.
