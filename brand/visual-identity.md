# Visual identity

Black and white are the primary identity colors. Functional accents express meaning: cyan for active guidance, green for improvement, orange for pain points, ivory for editorial readability and quiet neutral for reporting. Do not use functional colors as unexplained decoration or rely on color alone.

Inter supports interface and explanatory copy. Source Serif Pro supports editorial brand statements. This allocation is provisional; see the conflict register.

Only source-extracted logo artwork is included. It is a raster reference, not a production master. Do not redraw the logo or invent clear-space and minimum-size claims.

Source: Visual Identity pp. 2–7; Brand Book pp. 26–30.
