Experience Intelligence

01 / Strategic foundation

Beyond ratings.
Find what fits.

Born in Abu Dhabi. Built for the world. An impartial, evidence-led and warm intelligence layer for the experience economy.

Source-grounded brand system. Provisional design decisions remain visible, so the system can evolve with confidence.

Purpose

To make complex experience information meaningful, comparable and useful for every person, business and city.

Mission

To transform fragmented ratings, reviews, data and feedback into impartial, explainable intelligence that helps people choose better and organizations improve faster.

Vision

One universal standard for how experience quality is measured. Universal in method. Local in judgement.

One platform. Three outcomes.

FIND

Discover suitability by need, mood, criteria and context.

IMPROVE

Turn feedback into focused, evidence-led action.

CITY BENCHMARK

Compare experience quality across equivalent criteria.

04 / Verbal identity

Fact-led. Human-felt.

Impartial · Objective · Attuned · Discerning · Global · Actionable

Use

Right fit. Relevant criteria. Impartial evidence. Explainable recommendation. City benchmark. Experience improvement.

Avoid

Universal superiority, perfect objectivity, AI hype and unexplained judgments.

Experience Fit Score
Overall contextual suitability for the selected need or criteria set.
Confidence
Reliability based on signal quality, recency and consistency.
Trade-off
A factor that may reduce fit for some users even when the overall score is high.
Improvement Priority
A business-facing action area with recurring evidence behind it.
City Benchmark
Comparison across equivalent criteria, not a judgment of cultural superiority.

A living source of truth.
